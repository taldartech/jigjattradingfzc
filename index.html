About Us

Who We Are

WE ARE Jig Jat

Welcome to Jig Jat Trading FZC, your trusted partner in the world of international trade. We are a leading import and export trading, marketing, and distribution company headquartered in the vibrant city of Dubai, strategically positioned at the crossroads of global commerce.

  • Our Mission
  • Our Vision
  • About Us

The mission of the company is to consistently deliver the safest, high quality and most potent products and services using highly motivated and competent human capital with an incredible passion for innovation, excellence, and superior customer service.

Our vision is to offer our customers advanced, integrated technologies and a strong manufacturing and distribution scale across our strategic global network through strategic business planning and execution.

We operate largely in sub-Saharan Africa and are specialists in the B2B distribution of consumer electronics, particularly white goods, audio/visual appliances, and kitchen appliances. In our seven years of operation, Jig Jat has built an extensive network of customers and suppliers worldwide, covering all aspects of import/export with speed and efficiency using the most cost-effective methods. While Jig Jat Trading FZC at inception focused more on the marketing and distribution of Home Appliances, it has, in time, increased its range to cover ventures in rubber and plastics manufacturing, canned food production, and even battery making in attempts to capture a significant share of the African market with its own brands Jig Jat Trading FZC remains committed to providing our customers with the best possible products and services at the most competitive prices.
